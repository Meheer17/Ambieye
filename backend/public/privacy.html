⚠️ Important Medical & Wellness Disclaimer
DementiCare is a digital companion and assistive care coordination tool designed to facilitate cognitive engagement, daily memory activities, and communication between patients, family caregivers, and supervising healthcare professionals. DementiCare does NOT provide medical diagnosis, clinical advice, or emergency treatment. It is not a medical device. Always seek the advice of a qualified physician or healthcare provider regarding any medical condition or emergency.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Meheer J ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, protects, and handles personal and sensitive user data when you access or use the DementiCare mobile application ("App") and associated services (together, the "Service").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your personal privacy and maintaining transparent, secure handling of health-related and caregiver coordination information in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global privacy regulations.

By downloading, registering for, or using DementiCare, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver assistive dementia care features, daily routines, and family/caregiver coordination, DementiCare collects the following categories of information:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • User Credentials: Full name, username, email address, password hash, and designated account role (Patient, Caregiver, or Doctor).
  • Demographic Information: Age, date of birth, gender, and language preference to adapt cognitive exercise difficulty.
  • Caregiver & Emergency Contact Details: Names, phone numbers, and relationships of family members, primary caregivers, or emergency contacts entered to enable care alerts.

B. Cognitive Activities & Care Management Data

  • Cognitive Exercise Results: Scores, completion times, accuracy metrics, and response trends from memory games and cognitive stimulation activities.
  • Daily Routine & Reminders: Schedules, medication intake reminders, hydration prompts, and daily checklist confirmations.
  • Consultation & Care Queries: Messages, notes, symptom logs, or questions submitted by patients/caregivers to their designated healthcare provider through the App.
  • Doctor Clinical Records: Visit summaries, medical guidance notes, and progress observations recorded by authorized healthcare professionals.

C. Device, Sensor & Technical Information

  • Device Attributes: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status.
  • App Performance & Crash Telemetry: Anonymized application logs, error traces, and performance diagnostics used solely to resolve bugs and ensure system stability.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose Specification

DementiCare only requests runtime device permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core features. In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, our usage of these permissions is detailed below:

  • CAMERA Camera Permission: Used exclusively for real-time visual tracking during interactive cognitive/memory exercises (such as eye movement coordination, gaze interaction, and visual engagement verification) and for optional user profile pictures.
    Important: Visual frames and video feeds are processed in real-time on your device or securely via our server for immediate exercise feedback. We do not record, sell, or commercially store personal camera feeds for any advertising or surveillance purpose.
  • MICROPHONE Microphone / Audio Permission: Used when the user engages in optional voice-assisted cognitive prompts, speech-based memory exercises, or recorded care notes. Audio data is used only for the active exercise session and is not accessed in the background.
  • NOTIFICATIONS Notification Permission: Used to deliver time-critical medication alerts, scheduled memory routine reminders, caregiver check-in prompts, and notifications when a doctor answers an inquiry. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data based on legitimate service delivery and explicit user consent for the following purposes:

  • Core App Functionality: Providing tailored cognitive stimulation activities, memory tracking, and daily schedule management.
  • Care Coordination: Allowing linked family caregivers and supervising doctors to monitor progress, receive alerts, and respond to queries.
  • Reminders & Alerts: Dispatching timely local or push notifications for medications and scheduled activities.
  • Platform Security & Maintenance: Authenticating user accounts, preventing unauthorized access, monitoring system reliability, and repairing software defects.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling statutory duties and responding to lawful governmental or court orders where mandatory.

Zero Targeted Advertising: DementiCare does not serve third-party advertisements. Your personal, health, and cognitive activity information is never sold, rented, leased, or traded to data brokers or advertising exchanges.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We strictly limit the sharing of your information to the following parties:

  • Authorized Care Circle: When you connect your account with a family caregiver or physician, your progress reports, queries, and activity logs are shared with those specific authorized individuals to support your care.
  • Trusted Technical Service Providers: We engage reputable cloud infrastructure providers (secure database hosting, cloud compute, notification delivery) who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements and data protection standards.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by a valid legal process, subpoena, or regulation, or to protect the vital life and safety of the user or others in an emergency.
  • No Commercial Sale: We do not sell user personal data to any third party for marketing or monetization purposes.

6. Data Security Practices

We implement robust organizational, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your sensitive information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the DementiCare app and backend servers are secured using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Role-Based Access Control: User data is partitioned by role (Patient, Caregiver, Doctor) and secured using cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Doctors and caregivers can only access records of patients explicitly linked to their care profile.
  • Password Protection: Passwords are encrypted using modern cryptographic hashing algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Server Hardening: Routine system audits, vulnerability mitigations, and automated security monitoring.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

Google Play requires developers to provide a clear data retention policy and a user-accessible path for deleting accounts and personal data:

  • Retention Period: We retain your personal profile and care data only for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill legitimate care coordination purposes. If an account is inactive for more than 24 months, it is slated for archival and deletion.
  • Full Account & Data Deletion: You retain complete ownership over your data and can request permanent account deletion at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your DementiCare account and all associated personal data via any of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account and confirm your request.
  2. Web-Based Deletion Portal: Visit our dedicated web deletion page at /delete-account, log in with your credentials, and click "Delete My Account".
  3. Email Request: Send an email directly to developer Meheer J at meheerj2327.sse@saveetha.com with the subject line "Delete DementiCare Account" including your registered username/email.

Scope of Deletion: Upon receiving your deletion request, your profile, authentication credentials, cognitive activity logs, game scores, and reminder history will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Target Audience: DementiCare is designed and intended for adults, seniors coping with cognitive conditions, family caregivers, and healthcare practitioners (aged 18 and older).

DementiCare is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has registered or provided personal information to DementiCare without verified parental/guardian consent, please contact us immediately at meheerj2327.sse@saveetha.com, and we will promptly remove such information and terminate the account.

9. User Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under GDPR, CCPA, or applicable state and national laws), you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right of Rectification: You can edit and correct your profile information directly in the App or request corrections.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request permanent deletion of your data as outlined in Section 7.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke device permissions (Camera, Microphone, Notifications) at any time through your mobile device operating system settings (Settings > Apps > DementiCare > Permissions).
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request an electronic copy of your activity and care data in a structured, commonly used format.
